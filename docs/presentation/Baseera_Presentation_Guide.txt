MOTTO: "Teaching minds to doubt, not machines to decide."

PROJECT SUMMARY:
Baseera is an AI-powered mobile application designed to cultivate critical 
thinking habits before sharing digital content. It acts as a personal digital 
literacy coach — analyzing news articles, social media claims, and images to 
uncover hidden emotional bias, urgency manipulation, and privacy risks using 
artificial intelligence.

Unlike traditional fact-checkers that deliver rigid verdicts, Baseera utilizes 
Socratic questioning: it turns whatever you share into an empowering reflection 
question, accompanied by a 20-second cognitive pause timer to interrupt 
impulsive viral sharing loops.

PART 3: DETAILED SCREEN-BY-SCREEN WALKTHROUGH (SCREENS 1 TO 9)
================================================================================

SCREEN 1: SPLASH SCREEN (Boot Sequence)
- Displayed upon launching the application.
- Highlights the Baseera eye insignia and brand motto: "Digital Literacy Coach".
- Executes device readiness and offline AI capability checks.
- Automatically transitions into the main experience after 2 seconds.

SCREEN 2: ONBOARDING SLIDE 1 (Share with Intention)
- First educational slide for new users.
- Promotes mindful sharing: encourages users to pause and verify facts before 
  reflexively reposting on social media.
- Includes pagination indicators and Skip / Next controls.

SCREEN 3: ONBOARDING SLIDE 2 (AI-Powered Analysis)
- Explains how Baseera inspects linguistic tone, emotional urgency triggers, 
  and source reliability.
- Deconstructs cognitive framing bias using multi-modal AI models.

SCREEN 4: ONBOARDING SLIDE 3 (Cultivate Critical Thinking)
- Introduces habit formation tracking and the weekly Digital Resilience Index.
- Clarifies that Baseera does not judge content, but sharpens natural reasoning.

SCREEN 5: HOME DASHBOARD (User Control Center)
- Displays dynamic greeting (Good morning / afternoon / evening) with user name.
- Prominent Hero CTA Card: "Analyze Content" with "Paste Link or Media" button.
- Weekly Summary Card: Displays total items checked and Habit Score (e.g., 88%).
- Quick Access Cards: "Privacy Scan" and "Fact History".
- Recent Observation Feed: Colored status badges ("High Accuracy", "Framing Bias").
- Bottom Navigation: Home, Analyze, Privacy, Reports.

SCREEN 6: CONTENT INPUT SCREEN (Share & Media Upload)
- Multimedia intake: Supports pasted URLs and uploaded images / screenshots.
- Clipboard Auto-Detection: Automatically detects copied links from other apps.
- Active Analysis Modules: Cognitive Bias, Fact Audits, Synthetic Media.
- Truth Protocol: Cross-references 280+ peer-reviewed sources and archives.
- Security Guarantee: End-to-end encrypted analysis with zero data retention.

SCREEN 7: PROCESSING ENGINE (Transparent State)
- Engaging multi-step progress indicator (e.g., "Step 2 of 4 • Verifying tone").
- Circular progress animation displaying active percentage.
- Real-time educational "Critical Mindset" tips to teach users during the brief wait.
- Encrypted processing badge reassuring user privacy.

SCREEN 8: AI ANALYSIS RESULT SCREEN (Socratic Reflection)
- Emotional Bias Gauge: Numerical percentage and label (e.g., 78% High Bias).
- Urgent Framing Classification: Highlights emotional manipulation triggers.
- Core Socratic Question: Tailored prompt (e.g., "Did this post show a reliable 
  source before asking you to share it?").
- 20-Second Cognitive Pause Timer: Breaks the impulsive sharing dopamine loop.
- Independent Verification Links: Direct connections to Reuters, AP, and WHO.

SCREEN 9: PRIVACY INSPECTION SCREEN (On-Device Shield)
- Evaluates photos before sharing to prevent unintended privacy leaks.
- Street Signs & Geo Markers: Detects storefronts and signs -> One-click Mask.
- Bystander Faces: Identifies non-consenting individuals -> One-click Blur All.
- EXIF GPS Metadata: Automatically strips precise latitude/longitude tags.
